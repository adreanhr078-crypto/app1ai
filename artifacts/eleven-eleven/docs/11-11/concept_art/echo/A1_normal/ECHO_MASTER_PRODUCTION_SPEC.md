## 1. Core Narrative & Psychological Identity
> *"Echo is NOT a hero, NOT a chosen one, NOT a fantasy protagonist. He is a forgotten ordinary person before becoming a monster. He must look like someone who had a normal life before it was taken away, not someone born broken."*

### Key Physical & Psychological Constraints:
* **Age & Build:** 19 years old. A young face bearing the weight of emotional exhaustion (NOT physically aged 25+). Lean, vulnerable, slightly slouched posture.
* **The Gaze:** Faded pale grey sanpaku eyes, looking downward or slightly away, avoiding aggressive direct eye contact.
* **The 15% Humanity:** A subtle, gentle trace of his former kindness remains—not an empty zombie, but someone whose past warmth is still faintly visible beneath deep fatigue.
* **The Unconscious Shield (The Left Eye Rule):** Heavy, jagged obsidian front hair falls to cover his left eye. This is an involuntary psychological defense mechanism hiding his vulnerability, not a decorative hairstyle.
* **The Frozen Anchor:** On his wrist is a worn, vintage mechanical watch permanently stopped at **11:11**—the tactile proof that his normal life ended in an instant.
